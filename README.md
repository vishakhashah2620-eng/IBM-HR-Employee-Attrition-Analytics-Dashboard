# IBM-HR-Employee-Attrition-Analytics-Dashboard
1. Project Headline / Title:-
HR Analytics Dashboard – Employee Attrition Analysis


2. Short Project Description:-
Developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Microsoft Excel to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, overtime, income, tenure, and department-wise employee turnover. The dashboard transforms HR data into meaningful insights to help identify attrition patterns, understand employee retention challenges, and support data-driven HR decision-making.

3. Tech Stack:-
  - Microsoft Excel
  - PivotTables & PivotCharts
  - Data Cleaning & Formatting
  - Data Analysis
  - KPI Metrics
  - Interactive Slicers
  - Dashboard Design & Data Visualization
  - Excel Formulas


4.Data Source:-
   Dataset: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance
   The dataset contains employee-level HR information, including:-
          -Employee demographics
          -Department and job role
          -Monthly income
          -Years at company
          -Job satisfaction
          -Overtime
          -Age
          -Promotion history
          -Employee attrition status
Dataset size shown in your dashboard: 1,470 employees


5. Business Problem:-
   1.Organizations face challenges understanding why employees leave, which departments experience higher attrition, and what factors may influence employee           retention.
   2.Without proper analysis, HR teams may struggle to identify employee groups with higher turnover, monitor overtime-related attrition, understand job-role
     patterns, and make informed workforce decisions.
   
Problem I solved:-
1. I solved the problem of HR data being difficult to understand and analyze in its raw format. By creating an interactive dashboard, I consolidated
   important   employee metrics into a single view, allowing HR teams to quickly monitor attrition, compare departments and job roles, and identify
   workforce  segments with higher employee turnover.
2.The dashboard makes HR data easier to interpret and helps management make informed decisions regarding employee retention and workforce planning.



6. Goal of the Dashboard:-The main goal was to:
1.Monitor overall employee attrition.
2.Compare active employees versus employees who left.
3.Identify attrition patterns by age group.
4.Analyze attrition across departments and job roles.
5.Explore the relationship between overtime and attrition.
6.Compare average income across education fields.
7.Provide interactive filters for deeper analysis.


7.Walkthrough of Key Visuals:-
1.KPI Summary-Displays total employees, employees who left, active employees, attrition rate, and average age to provide an overall workforce overview.
2.Attrition Share by Overtime-  Shows the overtime-related split displayed in the dashboard, helping explore overtime patterns alongside employee attrition.
3.Attrition Rate by Age Group-Compares attrition and retention percentages across different age groups to identify age segments with higher attrition.
4.Attrition by Job Role-Compares employees who left and stayed across job roles, highlighting roles with higher attrition counts.
5.Employee Attrition by Department-Analyzes attrition and retained employees across Sales, Research & Development, and Human Resources.
6.Average Monthly Income by Education Field & Attrition-Compares average monthly income across education fields for employees who left and those who remained.
